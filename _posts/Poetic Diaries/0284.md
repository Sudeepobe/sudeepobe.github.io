### A Fight with My Shadow

An early morning walking on the cliff,  
Even before a ray could touch the dew,  
A hungry beast trying to escape his belief,  
Yet he can't—even in absence of his shadow.  

A lucky dream pointing to his relief,  
A hollow prey, missed by his arrows.  
At his front, on his back,  
To his right, from his left,  
Every time he waves, she waves back.  

But then he noticed—  
She can only be on one side at a time.  
He laughed.  

A mixed feeling emerged,  
The sun’s hiding game began to end.  
He thought it was time she would merge,  
But a craving dark shadow crept on his back again.  

He fights again, blaming the sun for exposure,  
A cycle of blame stretching wider than ever.  

---

*Harsh truth.*
